Barntree

Your understanding outlives your code.

With Barntree you design a reusable Template, then create Constructions that follow it. Describe what each building block means, arrange leaves, lists and maps, and add real members to each Construction. Every saved change has a history. In a barn it works under the other tools, with no tab of its own.
